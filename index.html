questions

do i need an account?

nope. just pick a mode and play. your best scores are saved on this device only.

how do rooms work?

hit create a room to get a four letter code. your friends enter it under multiplayer, you press start, and everyone races through the same eight words.

how is scoring done?

every correct word is worth 100 points. in timed games you get up to 50 more for answering quickly, and each word in a row adds a streak bonus.

i can’t hear the word

check your volume and press hear again. words are read by your browser’s built in voice, so it needs a browser that supports speech.

 How to play

Press play and listen to the word. Type it letter by letter, then hit Enter. Correct answers earn 100 points plus a speed bonus.

In multiplayer, everyone gets the same words at the same time. Create a room, share the 4-letter code, and the host starts the round.
